Learning from Potential Earthquakes

  • Use early pressure data from $N_d=175$ seafloor sensors to forecast a longer QoI trajectory:

    \[ N_d=175,\qquad N_t^{\mathrm{obs}}=256,\qquad N_t^{\mathrm{pred}}=420 \]

    Assume the earthquake source has stopped after $N_t^{\mathrm{obs}}$, and zero-pad the source:

    \[ \mathbf{d}=\mathbf{F}\mathbf{m}_{1:N_t^{\mathrm{obs}}}+\boldsymbol{\eta}, \qquad \mathbf{q}=\mathbf{F}_q \operatorname{pad}_{N_t^{\mathrm{obs}}\to N_t^{\mathrm{pred}}}(\mathbf{m}) \]

  • Under a smoothing Matérn Gaussian prior on $\mathbf{m}$, the Gaussian reference posterior is:

    \[ \mathbf{q}\mid\mathbf{d}\sim \color{BF5700}{ \gamma_{\mathbf{d}}:= \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{d}, \boldsymbol{\Gamma}_{\mathrm{post}(q)})} \]

  • A more appropriate prior for the seafloor motion is governed by non-Gaussian rupture physics, $\mathbf{m}^{i}\sim p_{\mathrm{rand}}(\mathbf{m})$. Assuming only sample access to $p_{\mathrm{rand}}(\mathbf{m})$, yielding joint samples:

    \[ \begin{aligned} (\mathbf{d}^i,\mathbf{q}^i) & = (\mathbf{F}\mathbf{m}^i+\boldsymbol{\eta}^i, \mathbf{F}_q\mathbf{m}^i) \sim p_{\mathrm{rand}}(\mathbf{d},\mathbf{q}),\\ \color{BF5700}{\nu_{\mathbf{d}}} & \color{BF5700}{:=\mathrm{Law}_{\mathrm{rand}}(\mathbf{q}\mid\mathbf{d})} \quad \text{intractable in density} \end{aligned} \]

Goal

Learn conditional sampling from $\nu_{\mathbf{d}}$ using only joint samples of $(\mathbf{d}^i,\mathbf{q}^i)\sim p_{\mathrm{rand}}(\mathbf{d},\mathbf{q})$.
