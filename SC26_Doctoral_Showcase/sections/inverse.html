Bayesian framework for inverse problems

  • Inverse problem is ill posed: many parameter fields can fit the data
  • Bayesian inverse problem: characterize posterior distribution of parameter
    \[ \textcolor{#6aaee8}{\pi_{\text{post}}(m \mid d^{\text{obs}})} \;\textcolor{#f6b93b}{\propto\; \pi_{\text{like}}(d^{\text{obs}} \mid m)\; \pi_{\text{prior}}(m)} \]
    (Bayes’ Rule)
  • Gaussian prior and noise → posterior is also Gaussian
  • Computing the mean requires solving a \(\sim 10^9 \times 10^9\) dense linear system
  • Further issues: high effective rank, slow spectral decay, hyperbolic PDEs
    • Surrogates are not feasible
