Summary and future work

  • High-fidelity physics-based Bayesian OED at billion-parameter scale
  • Data-space formulation of OED objective
  • Pipelined, double-buffered I/O to overlap with GPU compute
  • Performance-portable, multi-GPU implementation scales excellently to 1,024 GPUs of varying architectures
  • Cascadia: 175 sensors selected from 600 in < 5 minutes on 16 GB200 GPUs
  • Verified solution vs. 10,000 random networks
  • Optimality bounds based on submodularity (63% → 87%)
  • Algorithms are extensible to more general OED problems
  • Next steps: goal-oriented and A-optimal design
